# FROST BYOK (Conduition) — structure-only reference

## Structural pattern demonstrated

This article opens by anchoring the concept to a dated external question — a quoted exchange from a public venue (Nostr) that motivates the investigation. The author cites the artifact (who asked, when, where) before proceeding to the functional definition. This gives the explanation a concrete provenance: the reader learns not just what the concept is, but what real question prompted its articulation. The dated reference occupies one or two sentences at the very top; it does not sprawl into a narrative digression. Once the question is on the page, the piece pivots cleanly into the technical definition.

## Why voice is NOT imported

Conduition's voice is blog-performative (aimed at Bitcoin-community engagement). Specific tells: prior-art paragraphs, "Let's dive in" openers, self-deprecating asides that perform personality rather than explain. None of these ports to explanatory clarity; the skill should extract the skeleton only.

## How to apply this pattern in a concept note

If the concept arises from a public Q&A (mailing list, GitHub discussion, Discord), open with a one-sentence reference to the dated artifact (e.g., "On 2025-06-03 in the bitcoin-dev mailing list, <person> asked: ...") before the functional definition. Keep the reference short; the concept is still the star.
